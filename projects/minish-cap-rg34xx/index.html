02 / WHY THIS APPROACH?

Why this approach?

↑ Index

A personal link to the GBA

I grew up with PC games. My father gave me my first console, a Game Boy Advance. I chose the RG34XX for its close physical resemblance to that handheld.

Its 720 × 480 display also fits the game's 240 × 160 image at exactly 3× scale. Each original pixel becomes a 3 × 3 block.

Restoration, not new content

The aim is to preserve the content, artwork, music, mechanics and original controls. Technical changes should help that same work run reliably.

Painting restoration is the guiding analogy: retain the image rather than add new details. A demonstrated technical defect can be addressed without redesigning the work.

A curated starting point

The player should not need dozens of adjustments. The reference policy favours original imagery, unobstructed gameplay and accessible menus.

Native execution and lower clocks are tools for this purpose. They are not reasons to add effects or claim unmeasured gains.

04 / WHAT DID THE TESTS SHOW?

What did the tests show?

↑ Index

The 7 September review used existing records. It did not run new device tests.

Timing: lower typical lateness

The p95 tick lateness fell from 5.791 to 0.028 ms in two archived captures. This means about 95% of recorded values were at or below those thresholds.

Delays still occurred. The largest presentation-call duration increased from 46.451 to 60.917 ms.

Audio: two different observations

An earlier machine test reported an audio regression. The later manual session received audio OK.

Both observations remain in the report. Listening acceptance does not erase the earlier result.

Lower clocks: a selected configuration

The accepted record uses CPU/GPU ceilings of 936/420 MHz. Lower energy use was a goal, not a measured result.

The final session has 874 monitor samples. Its highest recorded temperature was 52.6 °C.

These are session results, not full-game guarantees. No original-GBA gameplay-bug correction is established by the reviewed evidence.

Read sample sizes, exact values and open diagnostic questions

The timing pair contains 10,203 and 10,449 ticks, with one capture per variant. The observations are not independent test runs.
